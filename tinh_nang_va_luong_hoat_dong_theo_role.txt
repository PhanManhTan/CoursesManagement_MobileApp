============================================================
1. TONG QUAN ROLE VA DIEU HUONG
============================================================

He thong co 3 role chinh:
- student: hoc vien.
- instructor: giang vien.
- admin: quan tri vien.

Dang ky moi chi cho phep chon role student hoac instructor. Role admin duoc xu ly nhu tai khoan co san trong bang users.

Luong vao app:
1. SplashActivity ap dung ngon ngu da luu va hien progress.
2. Neu chua hoan tat onboarding thi vao OnboardingActivity.
3. Neu da onboarding va da co session hop le thi dieu huong theo role:
   - admin -> AdminMainActivity.
   - instructor -> InstructorMainActivity.
   - role khac hoac student -> HomeActivity.
4. Neu chua dang nhap thi vao LoginActivity.

Luong dang nhap:
1. Nguoi dung nhap email va mat khau.
2. App goi Supabase Auth signIn.
3. App lay profile that trong bang users gom role, full_name, email, bio, avatar_url, status.
4. Neu status la banned thi xoa session tam va khong cho vao app.
5. Neu hop le thi luu access token, refresh token, userId, role, profile cache.
6. Dieu huong theo role admin / instructor / student.

Luong dang ky:
1. Nguoi dung nhap ho ten, email, mat khau, xac nhan mat khau va chon role student/instructor.
2. App validate input: email hop le, mat khau toi thieu 6 ky tu, mat khau khop nhau.
3. App goi Supabase signUp kem metadata full_name va role.
4. App chuyen sang OTP.
5. Sau khi verify OTP dang ky thanh cong, app dua nguoi dung ve LoginActivity de dang nhap lai va lay role that tu bang users.

Luong quen mat khau:
1. Nguoi dung nhap email o ForgotPasswordActivity.
2. App goi recover de gui OTP recovery.
3. Nguoi dung nhap OTP o OtpVerifyActivity.
4. Neu OTP dung, app chuyen sang SetNewPasswordActivity.
5. Nguoi dung dat mat khau moi, app goi updatePassword.
6. App xoa session cu va dua ve LoginActivity.

Tinh nang dung chung:
- Quan ly session, token va profile cache bang SessionManager.
- Doi ngon ngu Anh/Viet bang LanguageManager trong man Account.
- Xem va cap nhat profile: ho ten, email, bio.
- Upload avatar len Supabase Storage va cap nhat avatar_url trong bang users.
- Dang xuat: clear session va quay ve LoginActivity.
- Nhan FCM push notification, tu dong upload FCM token theo userId.
- Danh sach notification rieng tung user, sap xep moi nhat truoc, bam notification de danh dau da doc.
- Badge so notification chua doc tren bottom navigation.

2. ROLE STUDENT - HOC VIEN
============================================================

Menu chinh cua student:
- Home.
- Search.
- My Courses.
- Notification.
- Account.

2.1. Home / Kham pha khoa hoc

Tinh nang:
- Hien thi loi chao theo email dang nhap.
- Hien thi danh muc khoa hoc.
- Hien thi danh sach khoa hoc noi bat/theo danh muc.
- Loc khoa hoc khong nen hien thi cho hoc vien: pending, review, rejected, draft, private, hidden, inactive.
- Khong hien thi lai cac khoa hoc hoc vien da enroll trong danh sach goi y.
- Hien badge gio hang theo so item trong cart.
- Lay va upload FCM token.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student dang nhap thanh cong va vao HomeActivity.
2. HomeViewModel load categories va courses.
3. Student chon mot danh muc hoac "All Courses".
4. App lay courses theo category hoac tat ca courses.
5. App loc khoa hoc visible voi student va loai bo khoa hoc da enroll.
6. Student bam vao course item.
7. App mo CourseDetailActivity voi COURSE_ID.

2.2. Search / Tim kiem khoa hoc

Tinh nang:
- Tim khoa hoc theo tu khoa.
- Loc theo category.
- Ket hop tu khoa + category.
- Hien thong tin title, gia, thumbnail va ten instructor.
- Chi hien courses co status public/approved/published/active; an pending/rejected/draft/private/hidden/inactive.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student vao tab Search.
2. App load danh muc va tao cac nut category.
3. Student nhap tu khoa, chon category hoac chon All.
4. Nut submit chi enable khi dieu kien tim kiem thay doi.
5. App goi CourseRepository search/getByCategory/searchByCategoryAndTitle.
6. App loc course visible voi student.
7. Student bam course trong ket qua.
8. App mo CourseDetailActivity.

2.3. Course Detail / Chi tiet khoa hoc

Tinh nang:
- Xem thong tin khoa hoc: title, description, thumbnail, category, price/discount price.
- Xem thong tin instructor: ten, role, avatar.
- Xem curriculum theo chapter.
- Xem danh sach review va so luong review.
- Kiem tra da enroll hay chua.
- Them course vao gio hang.
- Mua ngay/enroll course qua CheckoutActivity.
- Neu da enroll thi hien trang thai Owned va an nut Add to Cart.
- Neu user khong phai student thi an nut mua/cart.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student mo CourseDetailActivity tu Home/Search/CourseList.
2. App load course theo COURSE_ID.
3. App load instructor, category, chapters va reviews.
4. App check enrollment cua user voi course.
5. Neu chua enroll:
   - Student bam Add to Cart -> app kiem tra cart hien tai -> neu chua co thi them vao cart.
   - Student bam Enroll/Buy -> app tao checkout payload gom COURSE_ID, gia tien va mo CheckoutActivity.
6. Neu da enroll:
   - App doi nut thanh Owned, disable mua lai va an Add to Cart.

2.4. Cart / Gio hang

Tinh nang:
- Xem danh sach course trong gio hang.
- Xem tong tien.
- Xoa tung course khoi gio hang.
- Bo qua cart item co course data khong con hop le.
- Chuyen sang checkout voi danh sach cartIds va courseIds.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student bam icon cart tu Home/CourseDetail.
2. CartActivity load cart theo userId.
3. App tinh tong tien bang discountPrice cua tung course.
4. Student co the xoa item.
5. Student bam Checkout.
6. App chuyen sang CheckoutActivity kem TOTAL_AMOUNT, CART_IDS, COURSE_IDS.

2.5. Checkout va thanh toan VNPAY

Tinh nang:
- Checkout course co phi qua VNPAY.
- Enroll mien phi voi course tong tien <= 0.
- Xac thuc lai gia course moi nhat truoc khi thanh toan.
- Neu gia thay doi, hien dialog cho phep tiep tuc voi gia moi.
- Tao payment URL qua Supabase Edge Function vnpay/create-url.
- Mo WebView thanh toan VNPAY.
- Xu ly return URL va goi Edge Function vnpay/ipn de verify.
- Tao notification thanh toan/enrollment thanh cong.
- Chuyen sang PaymentResultActivity.

Luong hoat dong:
1. CheckoutActivity nhan TOTAL_AMOUNT, CART_IDS, COURSE_IDS.
2. Neu totalAmount <= 0:
   - Student bam Enroll for Free.
   - App tao Enrollment cho tung course.
   - App xoa cac cart item lien quan.
   - App tao notification thanh cong va mo PaymentResultActivity.
3. Neu totalAmount > 0:
   - Student bam Pay Now.
   - App lay lai gia moi nhat cua tung course.
   - Neu tong gia khac tong ban dau, app hien dialog Price Update.
   - App goi Edge Function tao VNPAY URL.
   - App mo VNPAYActivity WebView.
   - Khi VNPAY redirect ve return URL, app verify va goi processSuccessfulPayment.
   - App tao notification va mo PaymentResultActivity.

2.6. My Courses / Khoa hoc cua toi

Tinh nang:
- Xem danh sach khoa hoc da enroll.
- Hien progress: so bai da hoan thanh / tong bai va phan tram.
- Loc All / Ongoing / Completed.
- Mo LearningActivity de hoc.
- Chi hien nut Review khi progress dat 100%.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student vao tab My Courses.
2. App load enrollments theo userId.
3. App hien danh sach course da mua/khoa hoc mien phi da enroll.
4. Student chon filter:
   - All: tat ca enrollments.
   - Ongoing: course chua hoan thanh.
   - Completed: course da hoan thanh.
5. Student bam course -> mo LearningActivity voi COURSE_ID va COURSE_TITLE.
6. Neu course complete 100%, student bam Review -> mo ReviewActivity.

2.7. Learning / Hoc bai

Tinh nang:
- Xem video bai hoc bang ExoPlayer.
- Xem danh sach chapter/lesson.
- Chon lesson de hoc.
- Theo doi tien do hoc video va luu lesson_progress.
- Danh dau lesson hoan thanh khi progress dat nguong hoan thanh.
- Hien title lesson hien tai.
- Tab Discussion: xem comment theo lesson, them comment, reply comment.
- Tab Quiz: hien cau hoi quiz cua lesson, nop quiz, reset quiz.
- Tab Files: hien file tai lieu dinh kem va download bang DownloadManager.
- Tab Lessons: quay lai danh sach bai hoc.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student mo LearningActivity tu My Courses.
2. App load chapters theo COURSE_ID.
3. Voi tung chapter, app load lessons.
4. Voi tung lesson, app load lesson_progress theo userId + lessonId.
5. App gom du lieu thanh danh sach chapter/lesson kem trang thai unlock/progress.
6. Student chon lesson.
7. App load videoUrl vao ExoPlayer, cap nhat lesson title, load file/quiz/comment theo tab hien tai.
8. Trong khi video chay, app track position/duration va luu progress vao lesson_progress.
9. Neu dat nguong hoan thanh, app cap nhat completed_at va progress.
10. Student co the thao luan, lam quiz hoac tai file cua lesson.

2.8. Review khoa hoc

Tinh nang:
- Chi cho phep review neu user da enroll course.
- Kiem tra review da ton tai theo userId + courseId.
- Neu da review, khoa UI va hien review cu.
- Neu chua review, cho nhap rating va comment.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student bam Review trong My Courses khi course da complete.
2. ReviewActivity check enrollment.
3. App check review da co hay chua.
4. Neu chua co, student chon rating va nhap comment.
5. App insert Review.
6. Neu da co, app hien rating/comment va disable submit.

2.9. Notification va Account

Tinh nang:
- Xem notification ca nhan.
- Bam notification de mark as read.
- Badge notification chua doc.
- Xem/cap nhat profile, avatar, bio.
- Doi ngon ngu Anh/Viet.
- Dang xuat.

Luong hoat dong:
1. Student vao tab Notification hoac Account.
2. App load du lieu theo userId tu session.
3. Cac thay doi profile/avatar/ngon ngu duoc luu vao Supabase/users hoac SharedPreferences.
4. Dang xuat se clear session va quay ve LoginActivity.

5. CAC LUONG DU LIEU CHINH GIUA CAC ROLE
============================================================

5.1. Instructor tao course -> Admin duyet -> Student hoc
1. Instructor tao course, chapter, lesson, video, file va quiz.
2. Course duoc luu voi status can duyet/pending hoac status hien co.
3. Admin vao Approvals de preview noi dung.

7. Student thanh toan/enroll.
